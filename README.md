# Regiment Ledger

Write a medieval muster ledger and letters of recommendation for your regiment, with painted portraits, a cursive signature and a wax seal.

**Open it here: https://teamdemivfxfish-dev.github.io/regiment-ledger/**

**Or download the Windows program:** [RegimentLedger-1.0.1-portable.exe](https://github.com/teamdemivfxfish-dev/regiment-ledger/releases/download/v1.0.1/RegimentLedger-1.0.1-portable.exe) (about 95 MB). Double-click it; there is nothing to install, and it works without internet. If your browser asks, choose **Keep**. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## How to use it

1. Fill in **The Regiment** on the left: the regiment's name, your Captain, where and when.
2. Under **Signature & Seal**, type your name in **Sign as** and pick a handwriting style.
3. Under **The Men**, click a name to open his card. Fill in his name, rank, craft and what you write of him.
   - **Upload picture** takes any screenshot. You can move, zoom, turn and mirror it, then press **Paint it**.
   - Tick **Write a letter recommending him** to give him his own letter of recommendation.
   - **+ Add a man** adds a new card.
4. Save your papers with **Save as image** or **Save as PDF** above each paper, or **Save all as one PDF**.

Everything you type is saved in your own browser. Nobody else can see or change your roster. To move it to another computer, use **Copy backup** and **Load backup**.
